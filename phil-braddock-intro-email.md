**What the system offers**

The form covers both Cleaning Supplies and First Aid products, organised into clear categories — Brushes & Handles, Cleaning Chemicals, Mops, Gloves, Hi Vis, and more — making it straightforward to find what's needed for each event. There's also a search bar for quick lookups.

As items are added, a live order summary builds automatically on the right-hand side with a full category breakdown, subtotal, VAT, and total — so overseers always have a clear picture of their order before submitting.

On submission, overseers choose their preferred payment method (BACS transfer, card payment, or purchase order), and a confirmation is sent to their inbox immediately. We then follow up to confirm delivery arrangements.

**Account portal**

After placing their first order, overseers receive a link to set up a personal account where they can view order history and re-order from a previous submission — useful for conventions that run on a regular cycle.

While the system is primarily intended for convention and circuit assembly overseers, it's been built in a way that congregations can also use it should they be directed to do so — so it works at all levels if needed.
